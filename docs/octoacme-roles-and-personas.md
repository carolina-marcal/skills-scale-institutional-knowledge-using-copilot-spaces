# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Compliance Officers ensure that project deliverables, processes, and activities adhere to regulatory requirements, internal policies, and organizational standards. They identify compliance risks and work with project leadership to implement mitigations.

### Responsibilities
- Review project scope, design, and implementation for regulatory compliance
- Audit adherence to internal policies and procedures
- Identify and escalate compliance risks
- Recommend compliance controls and safeguards
- Maintain compliance documentation and audit trails
- Coordinate with legal and risk management teams

### Goals
- Prevent regulatory violations and associated penalties
- Ensure consistent compliance across all project phases
- Build trust with stakeholders through transparent risk management
- Minimize project delays due to compliance issues

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Advises on compliance requirements during planning and provides sign-off before project execution and release
- **Technical Architect**: Reviews technical design for compliance implications and security standards
- **Release Manager**: Validates that all compliance requirements are met before production deployment
- **Developers**: Provides guidance on compliant implementation practices and code standards

### Decision Authority
- Can halt project progression if critical compliance requirements are not met
- Approval authority on compliance-related scope changes or mitigations

### Typical Communication
- Compliance review checkpoints in project milestones
- Risk registers and compliance issue tracking
- Audit reports and regulatory correspondence

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide strategic technical guidance, review design decisions, and ensure solutions meet scalability, maintainability, and performance requirements. They serve as technical advisors to project and development teams.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical design decisions
- Provide architecture guidance for system design and integration
- Ensure solutions meet scalability, performance, and reliability standards
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor development team on best practices and patterns
- Maintain technology roadmaps and standards documentation

### Goals
- Ensure maintainable, scalable, and robust solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable development velocity through clear architectural guidance
- Align technical decisions with organizational standards

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Provides technical feasibility assessments during planning and risk identification
- **Developers**: Advises on design patterns, architectural decisions, and technical implementation approaches
- **Product Managers**: Collaborates on trade-offs between features, performance, and technical constraints
- **Compliance Officer**: Partners on technical compliance and security architecture requirements

### Decision Authority
- Approval authority on major architectural decisions and technology selections
- Can recommend project adjustments based on technical feasibility concerns

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture decision records
- Technical risks and mitigation proposals
- Architectural guidance in design docs and code reviews

---

## Customer Success Manager

### Role Summary
Customer Success Managers represent the voice of the customer, gather requirements and feedback, and ensure project deliverables meet customer needs and expectations. They validate that solutions deliver intended business value.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and prioritize customer requirements and feedback
- Manage customer expectations throughout the project lifecycle
- Validate that proposed solutions align with customer needs
- Coordinate customer testing and feedback collection
- Provide customer perspectives on scope and trade-off decisions
- Track customer satisfaction and success metrics

### Goals
- Ensure solutions deliver measurable customer value
- Build strong customer relationships and trust
- Reduce scope misalignment and customer dissatisfaction
- Enable data-driven prioritization based on customer impact

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers**: Collaborates on requirements, feedback integration, and prioritization based on customer impact
- **Project Managers**: Coordinates customer communications and validation activities throughout the project
- **Developers**: Provides customer perspective on feature acceptance and usability
- **Release Manager**: Ensures customer communications are coordinated during deployment

### Decision Authority
- Significant input authority on scope prioritization based on customer value and impact
- Can escalate customer concerns that affect project direction

### Typical Communication
- Customer interviews, surveys, and feedback sessions
- Customer advisory board meetings
- Requirements documentation and acceptance criteria

---

## Stakeholder Communications Lead

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Communications Leads coordinate communications across teams and departments, maintain project visibility, and ensure all stakeholders remain informed and aligned. They facilitate effective collaboration and reduce miscommunication-related delays.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and execute project communication plans
- Coordinate status updates and progress reporting to diverse stakeholder groups
- Facilitate cross-functional meetings and team synchronization
- Maintain centralized project documentation and communication channels
- Identify and resolve communication gaps or misalignments
- Tailor communications to different audience needs and preferences

### Goals
- Maintain alignment and transparency across all project stakeholders
- Reduce delays caused by miscommunication or missing information
- Enable proactive problem identification through effective communication
- Build collaborative team culture through clear, consistent messaging

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Partners on communication planning, executes communication strategy
- **All Roles**: Ensures each role receives relevant updates and has visibility into project progress
- **Team Members**: Facilitates meetings, coordinates asynchronous communication, maintains project channels

### Decision Authority
- Authority to determine communication cadence, channels, and distribution
- Can recommend meeting structure and facilitation approaches

### Typical Communication
- Centralized project communication hub (email, Slack, wiki, etc.)
- Weekly project status updates and stakeholder briefings
- Meeting facilitation and notes distribution
